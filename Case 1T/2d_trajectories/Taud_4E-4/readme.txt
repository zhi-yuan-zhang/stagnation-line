This folder shows trajectories of specified particles in transient flow field and corresponding streamlines in time-averaged flow field at taud=4E-4.
The trajectories are traced both forward and backward from same locaitons but at different starting moments, i.e., from t=0P,t=0.25P,t=0.5P and t=P.
It supports Fig. 2c in the main text and Fig. S3 in the supporting information.
